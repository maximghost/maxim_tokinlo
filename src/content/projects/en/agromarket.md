## The project

AgroMarket lets farmers sell their produce online, directly to consumers. With no middleman, farmers keep control of their prices, and consumers know where their food comes from.

The project was built for the **Application Ergonomics** course: every screen had to stay simple and clear, including for people who rarely shop online.

## My role

- **The back end**, in Node.js with a MongoDB database: the server logic and the storage of products, accounts and orders.
- **The checkout flow**, from choosing products to confirming the order.
- **The tests**, to make sure that flow works end to end.

## What I took away

This project was my first hands-on work with MongoDB. It also taught me that a checkout flow is only reliable once it is tested: every customer goes through it, and a single bug is enough to lose a sale.
