## The game

Harvest World is a 2D platformer that runs in the browser. The player runs, jumps and dodges obstacles through the level, then faces a boss. Defeating it unlocks the next level.

## My role

In a team of five students, I owned **the player character**:

- **movement**: running, jumping and dodging, everything the player feels with each key press;
- **the weapon system**, which lets the character take on enemies and the end-of-level boss.

## What I took away

In a platformer, the character’s movement is what makes the game fun: one sluggish jump or imprecise dodge, and the game turns frustrating. This project also taught me to build a central piece that has to fit with the work of four other people.
