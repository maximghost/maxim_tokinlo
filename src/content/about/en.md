## My story

My name is Maxim Tokinlo. I am a product engineer based in Benin, and the founder of Ghost.Technologies.

In 2022, I started a bachelor’s degree in Internet and Multimedia at IFRI (University of Abomey-Calavi). Then life took another turn: I became a father. I chose to pause my studies to be there for my child and to provide for my family.

Those two years were not a standstill. It was during that time that I truly learned to build: on my own, I developed a business management application that I have been working on since 2023, then Gamberge, an app of ten brain games with online duels and mobile payments. With no classes and no mentor, I learned to see things through: design, code, test, start again.

Today, I am back at university, balancing my studies, my projects and my family life. This path taught me what no course does: organisation, perseverance and a sense of responsibility.

## How I work

**The problem before the technology.** I always start by understanding who will use what I build, and in what reality. On Gestatio, that means designing for women who do not always have a smartphone or network coverage.

**Rigour as the standard.** A product that works "most of the time" is not enough for me. I test, review and map the edge cases before calling a feature done.

**Stepping back when stuck.** Facing Gamberge's hardest problem, online duel matchmaking, I stopped fixing bugs one by one and redesigned the whole flow. That has become my method.

**AI as a tool, not a pilot.** I use artificial intelligence as a development assistant. Design decisions, architecture and code validation remain mine.

## Ghost.Technologies

Ghost.Technologies is the company through which I design my products. Its ambition: to create useful digital tools, built for African realities and ready to serve beyond them, in learning through play, health and business management.

## What drives me

I am competitive, but first with myself: each project must be better than the last. I want to build things that matter, and show that it can be done from Benin, at the highest level.
