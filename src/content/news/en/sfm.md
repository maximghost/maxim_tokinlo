## The context

SFM is a company based in Hêvié, Abomey-Calavi, selling premium wines and spirits, perfumes and fragrances, and groceries. It runs two shops. Its website has to present the brand, show the breadth of its range and let customers book a product without friction.

## What I am building

- **A showcase**: the brand, its three ranges and its two shops.
- **A catalogue**: products organised by range and category, designed mobile first.
- **Booking through WhatsApp**: from a product page, the customer opens a conversation with the shop, with the product already named in the message.
- **The visual identity**: brand guidelines, typography and mock-ups worthy of a premium retailer.

## Technical choices

- **Next.js and TypeScript**: pages rendered on the server or generated ahead of time, for a fast, well-indexed site, and fully typed code.
- **Supabase (PostgreSQL)**: the catalogue (products, categories, prices, availability) lives in a database, not in the code. It can change without redeploying the site.
- **WhatsApp**: the channel customers already use. No account to create, and no online payment to integrate at launch.

## Next steps

Validate the brand guidelines and the mock-up with the client, then the booking flow. Development comes next.
