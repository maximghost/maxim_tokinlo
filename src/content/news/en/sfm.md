Ghost.Technologies is building the website of **SFM**, a company based in Hêvié (Abomey-Calavi) that sells premium wines and spirits, perfumes and fragrances, and groceries across two shops. The site combines a showcase, a catalogue and a booking system through WhatsApp.

I handle everything, from the visual identity to the development. We are currently validating the brand guidelines, the mock-up and the booking flow with the client.
